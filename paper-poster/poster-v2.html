Paper and card recycling

What goes in
the paper bin

Clean, dry paper and card only. Flatten every box before it goes in.

Put these in Yes

Newspapers
Cardboard boxes
Paper bags
Envelopes and mail
Cereal and food boxes
Egg cartons
Magazines and flyers
Office paper and notebooks
Toilet and kitchen roll tubes
Scrap and shredded paper

Keep these out No

Greasy pizza boxesFood and oil spoil the batch
Coffee cupsPlastic lining inside
Used tissuesPaper towels too
Till receiptsShiny thermal paper

Not sure? Put it in the general bin.
